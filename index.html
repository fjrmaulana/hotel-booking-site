A white house with room to breathe

Thick walls keep the rooms cool, tall windows keep them bright, and the courtyard in the middle keeps everything quiet.

We are a family of hosts, not a chain. That means the person who answers your WhatsApp message is the person who will meet you at the door, and the breakfast is cooked to order rather than kept warm.
